## Coverage Label Rubric

- `strong`: unit-specific tests exercise the Pass 1 control surface directly; examples may reinforce usage, but examples alone never drive `strong`.
- `partial`: unit-specific evidence exists, but Pass 1 control-surface coverage is incomplete or only indirect.
